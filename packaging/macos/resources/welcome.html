Meldnet

Installs the menu bar app, command-line client and embedded WireGuard daemon for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or newer.

macOS will request administrator authorization. The daemon runs as root to manage VPN interfaces, routes and private DNS. The menu app runs as your ordinary desktop account.

Only the logged-in desktop account is granted local VPN control on first installation. Upgrades preserve the previously authorized account. Log into the intended account before installing.

No restart is required. Shut down any previous foreground Meldnet daemon yourself before installing. The installer loads the background service and menu app without stopping existing processes. Existing identity, enrollment and recovery state are preserved.
